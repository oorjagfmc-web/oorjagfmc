What We Do

Advanced Centre of Gastroenterology, Hepatology & Maternal-Fetal Medicine


Expert care for your digestive, liver and pregnancy needs delivered with precision, compassion and modern medical excellence.

Gastroenterology Services

Digestive issues

Diagnosis and management of acidity, gas, bloating, constipation, Irritable Bowel Syndrome and other digestive problems.

Liver diseases

Comprehensive evaluation and treatment of fatty liver, hepatitis, jaundice, cirrhosis and other liver disorders.

Obesity solutions

Personalized weight management planning including diet, lifestyle, medical and endoscopic support for best long-term results.

Pancreatobiliary Disorders

Specialized prenatal diagnostic procedures for early and accurate diagnosis of genetic and chromosomal conditions.

GI Oncology

Specialized screening and surveillance for early diagnosis and management of gastric and colorectal cancers.

Advanced Endoscopy/ ERCP/ EUS

Minimally invasive diagnosis and treatment of problems in GI tract, pancreas, liver and gall bladder with precision and care.

Fetal Medicine Services

3D/ 4D Ultrasonography

Advanced imaging that shows real-life movements and expressions of your baby in the womb.

Multiple Pregnancy Management

Detailed monitoring of twin and triplet pregnancies as to ensure a safer journey for the mother as well the babies.

High Risk Pregnancy Counselling

Counselling for women requiring special care due to maternal health conditions, previous pregnancy complications or fetal concerns.

Invasive Fetal Testing Procedures

Specialized procedure for early diagnosis of genetic and chromosomal conditions.

Fetal Echocardiography

Detailed structural and functional evaluation of the baby’s heart.

Fetal Neurosonography

Specialized scan of the baby’s brain for monitoring neurological development.

About Us

A dedicated super-speciality centre for digestive health and fetal medicine services.

Led by Dr Harsh Jain and Dr Ishita Agarwal, the centre focuses on creating a trusted healthcare environment where patients can receive personalised attention, evidence based treatment and ethical medical care. From managing digestive and liver disorders to specialized fetal imaging and high risk pregnancy evaluation, our goal is to provide modern, reliable and accessible healthcare under one roof.

AIIMS-trained doctors with experience from premiere institutes like Medanta and Apollo Hospitals.

State-of-the-art Ultrasound Machine for high definition pregnancy sonography.

⁠ Endoscopy suite equipped with image enhancement for accurate diagnosis and timely management.

⁠Daycare facility for endoscopic procedures and treatments requiring minimal hospital stay.

⁠Admission facility for patients requiring hospitalization and advanced medical support.

⁠24x7 on-call emergency services for patients in need.
